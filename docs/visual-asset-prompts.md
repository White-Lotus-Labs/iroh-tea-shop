# Tea room visual assets

These project assets were made with the built-in `image_gen` tool. The user-provided screenshot was a style and character reference; `tea-back-wall.jpg` was an additional style reference for the right wall. The screenshot itself is not served by the app.

## `public/images/tea-right-wall.jpg`

> Create a NEW project-ready photorealistic texture image for the RIGHT INTERIOR WALL of the same Japanese tea shop shown in the reference images. Use the references for matching warm dusk light, aged dark timber, amber plaster, handcrafted details, and natural cinematic realism. Output is a wide, straight-on architectural wall elevation with no room floor, no ceiling, no human, no counter and NO shelves (a separate real 3D shelf will stand in front of it). Show richly weathered amber clay plaster framed by dark wooden posts, a softly backlit shoji paper panel with subtle branch shadows toward the right/front side, a modest hanging indigo tea-shop fabric banner with a simple leaf emblem toward one end, restrained ceramic and ikebana hints in small recessed niches, and a warm paper lantern; all integrated as if a real lived-in tea shop. Deep, warm natural shadows, believable aged surfaces and tactile materials, no cartoon, no illustration, no text or lettering, no fisheye perspective. Keep the central section fairly calm so 3D shelving in front stays readable. Wide 16:9 composition, edge-to-edge wall artwork, high detail.

## `public/images/tea-host-face.jpg`

> Create a NEW square photorealistic FACE ALBEDO TEXTURE for a 3D character model, inspired by the elderly Japanese tea host in the reference image. This is an unlit orthographic frontal skin texture to wrap onto a sculpted 3D head, NOT a portrait in a room. Symmetric centered elderly Japanese man's face from forehead to chin, kind gently smiling expression, softly narrowed brown eyes, authentic fine age wrinkles near eyes and forehead, subtle age spots, natural warm tan skin with pores and realistic tonal variation. Face fills central 60% of canvas with clear margin at all sides; extend skin color out to all four edges so it can wrap smoothly. No hair, no beard, no moustache, no ears, no clothing, no background, no text, no hard lighting, no cast shadows, no cartoon, no painted or game-art style. Diffuse/albedo texture only: evenly lit, realistic detail, high resolution, square composition.

## `public/images/kimono-weave.jpg`

> Generate a NEW square seamless tileable PBR-style diffuse texture of traditional Japanese kimono fabric inspired by the elderly tea host's clothing in the reference. Close-up macro of natural handwoven silk-linen blend with very fine uneven fibers and subtle slubs, a neutral warm grey-beige base so a 3D material can tint it olive or umber, soft restrained texture, no folds, no seams, no pattern, no embroidery, no shadow, no lighting gradient, no objects, no text. Flat evenly lit albedo texture, photorealistic material detail, edges must tile seamlessly in both directions, 1024x1024 square.

## `public/images/tea-host-beard.png`

> Create a NEW isolated facial-hair texture asset for the elderly Japanese tea host. Output a realistic white-and-silver moustache flowing into a short, softly tapered chin beard, matching the reference host's warm smile and age. Front orthographic view, fine individual hair fibers and layered soft locks, natural uneven silhouette, subtle silver shadows, no exaggerated spikes. The asset contains ONLY the beard and moustache; NO head, skin, eyes, ears, hair on scalp, clothing, props, text, or scene. Center the beard in a square canvas with generous transparent margins, and use a genuinely transparent alpha background. Photorealistic hair material, diffuse even light, fit for applying to the curved chin of a three-dimensional character.

## `public/images/tea-host-robe.png`

> Create a NEW photorealistic diffuse texture/cutout for the FRONT OF A SEATED ELDERLY JAPANESE TEA HOST'S KIMONO, closely matching the reference man's clothing. Orthographic straight-on garment only from neck/collar to lap, filling the center of a square canvas: layered warm ochre-brown inner kimono with natural crossed lapels, loose dark olive-brown outer haori, woven fabric with subtle visible grain, draped realistic folds and an understated sash. The torso is seated, broad around the middle, slightly tapered at shoulder line; no arms or hands, no head, no skin, no beard, no legs, no environment, no props, no letters. Genuinely transparent background around the garment with soft clean contour. Natural cinematic realism and tactile cloth, even diffuse lighting, no cartoon, no illustration.
